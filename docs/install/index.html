Install the RCT Power Panel firmware

Flashing a blank Guition ESP32-S3 4848S040 over USB, from the browser. No software to install on your computer.

This erases everything on the panel. Wi-Fi credentials, the 24 h history on the SD card and every setting are gone. Use it to install onto a new or blank panel — not to update a panel that is already running. An update goes through the panel's own update page, behind the maintenance code.

What you need

  • Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge or Firefox — not Safari, not on an iPhone or iPad.
  • A USB cable to the panel, on the port that carries data.
  • The panel's USB port: it is the one behind the lower cover, next to the SD card.

After the flash

  1. The panel starts its own Wi-Fi network, RCT-Panel, with no password. Join it from your computer or phone.
  2. A configuration page opens. Enter the network the panel should live in, and the inverter's address. The panel then reboots and connects.
  3. From here the panel is reached at http://192.168.1.228, or at http://rct-panel.local from a browser with mDNS.

If something goes wrong

  • The browser does not offer the port. A dialog asking which port to use has to appear. If it does not, the panel is not enumerating: try another USB cable, another port, and make sure the cover is off.
  • The flash stops halfway. Run it again. Everything is erased first, so a half-finished attempt leaves nothing to recover.
  • Nothing happens after the flash. Press the reset button once. The panel boots the firmware it was given.
